MISSION BRIEFING
YOU ARE INVISIBLE UNTIL THEY FIND YOU

Every patrol runs a sensor. Inside its cone, with line of sight, a detection meter fills — the closer and louder you are, the faster. Halfway it breaks off to INVESTIGATE; full and it ALERTS, radios its pack and raises the sector ALARM. Break line of sight, run cold, and the hunt stands down.

SIGNATURE IS THE WHOLE GAME

Speed is noise, a hot cannon is a beacon, boost is a flare — all of it feeds the SIGNATURE bar, and signature is literally how far every sensor on the map reaches. The cones drawn on the floor are live: throttle down and watch them shrink. Speed is armor in a fight and noise on the prowl. Choose which tank to be.

Two shapes are drawn under every patrol and you have to respect both. The wedge is what it is looking at. The ring at its feet is what it can hear — inside that, facing stops mattering and it notices you whichever way it is pointed. Both shrink when you do.

KILL THEM BEFORE THEY KNOW

A shell that lands on a hull which never saw you is an AMBUSH — triple damage, so one cold shot deletes a patrol from across the map. A hull that dies without ever alerting pays a SILENT KILL bonus, and a boost-ram is the quietest kill in the game. A survivor sounds the alarm, so commit.

THE HEAT CANNON

There is no ammo — there is HEAT. Past the redline ticks the gun fires faster and harder; past the top it locks up. Tap R to vent: a marker sweeps the bar and a second tap inside the band is a PERFECT VENT — instant clear plus supercharged shells. Heat is signature, so venting is also how you go dark.

UPLINKS, THEN THE GATE

Clip a zone ring once — a drive-by is enough — and the hack runs itself while you are already gone. It pulses, and the noise pulls patrols in. When the last uplink falls the whole sector wakes up and an EXTRACTION GATE opens: the getaway is the finale. Reach it having never tripped the alarm for a GHOST EXTRACTION.

STYLE PAYS, GREED DECIDES

The combo runs on variety — repeat one kill method and the chain cools; mix cannon, ram, grenade, mine and shockwave and it climbs to ×5, which also multiplies your TECH income. Kill score does not bank: it rides an at-risk POT that spills 30% every time you take a hit and cashes out when you secure a zone.
