MISSION BRIEFING
THEY CAN'T SEE YOU YET

Each patrol has a vision cone and a hearing ring, both drawn on the ground. Inside them, with line of sight, its detection meter fills. Halfway, it comes to look. Full, it alerts its pack and the sector ALARM goes up. Break line of sight and stay quiet and the alarm times out.

STAY QUIET

Speed, a hot cannon and boost all raise your SIGNATURE, and signature is how far enemy sensors reach. Slow down and the cones on the ground shrink.

STRIKE FIRST

A shell that hits a hull that hasn't seen you does triple damage. A kill that never alerted pays extra. A boost-ram is the quietest kill. Anything that survives a hit raises the alarm.

THE CANNON RUNS ON HEAT

No ammo, just HEAT. Near the top the gun fires faster and harder; at the top it locks up. Tap R to vent, and tap again inside the band for a PERFECT VENT. Venting also makes you quieter.

HACK, THEN LEAVE

Drive through an uplink ring once to start its hack. It finishes on its own, but it makes noise. When the last one falls the whole sector wakes up and an EXTRACTION GATE opens. Hold it to leave. Never trip the alarm for a GHOST EXTRACTION bonus.
